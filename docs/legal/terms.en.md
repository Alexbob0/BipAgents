## 8. What AI can and cannot do

- **Your agents are artificial intelligence systems, not people.** The app tells you so on first launch, with the "AI"
  badge next to their name, at the start of each Live session, and in notifications and the widget.
- Their replies are **generated by an AI model**. They can be **wrong, incomplete, outdated or inappropriate**, and a
  model can confidently make facts up.
- They are **not a substitute for professional advice** (health, finance, law…). The demo's agents (Coach,
  Budget…) are illustrations. Check what matters before acting; in an emergency, call emergency services (112 in
  Europe).
- **BipAgents is not a medical device and makes no diagnosis. It does not provide investment advice.** What an agent
  says about sleep, health, a budget or spending remains general information produced by an AI.
- The audio files your agents create, such as podcasts, carry in their metadata a machine-readable mark stating that
  they were generated by an AI.
- Kerfy Studio controls neither the model you chose nor what it produces.

## 9. Agents that act, with your OK

- Depending on the tools you give them, your agents can **act**: send emails, browse and fill in forms, handle
  files, control your smart home, run commands on your server.
- Before a sensitive action, the agent **asks for your approval** (Once, This session or Deny), in the app or from
  the notification. When BipAgents installs Hermes Agent, risky commands always ask you (a Hermes Agent you already had
  keeps its own settings), and the app does not offer "Always allow". **You decide: read the request before
  accepting.** You are responsible for what you approve.
- Depending on your Hermes Agent configuration and installed tools, some actions may happen without an approval
  request. What your agents read (web pages, emails, files) may contain hidden instructions meant to hijack them: keep
  approvals on and give each agent only the access it needs.
- The emails and messages your agents send to other people state by default that they were written by an AI agent,
  and on whose behalf. Do not remove this statement to make people believe a human wrote them.
