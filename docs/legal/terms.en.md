  conversations.** Its role is limited to publishing the app and, for notifications, running a relay to Apple and

## 8. Agents that act, with your OK

- Depending on the tools you give them, your agents can **act**: send emails, browse and fill in forms, handle
  files, control your smart home, run commands on your server.
- Before a sensitive action, the agent **asks for your approval** (Once, This session or Deny), in the app or from
  the notification. When BipAgents installs Hermes, risky commands always ask you (a Hermes you already had keeps its
  own settings), and the app does not offer "Always allow". **You decide: read the request before accepting.** You are responsible for what you approve.
- Depending on your Hermes configuration and installed tools, some actions may happen without an approval request.
  What your agents read (web pages, emails, files) may contain hidden instructions meant to hijack them: keep
  approvals on and give each agent only the access it needs.
