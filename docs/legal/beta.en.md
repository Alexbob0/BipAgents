# Beta terms

> Last updated: 12 November 2026.

The BipAgents public beta lets you try the app and the all-included offer before subscriptions open. It is provided by
Kerfy Studio, the publisher of BipAgents.

- **By invitation.** The public beta opens with an invitation code, valid once, within the limit of 100 places.
- **Free.** The beta costs nothing: no payment method is asked for and no purchase is possible in the app during the
  beta.
- **The AI credit offered.** 2 € of model use per person and per month, with a strict cap. Once this credit is used,
  your agents no longer call the model until the next month: nothing is charged to you. It cannot be sold or refunded.
- **Unused place.** Beta places are limited. If nobody writes to your agents for 7 days, your place is freed for
  someone else. Writing to your agents means sending a message, a voice note, a photo or a file to an agent, making a
  Live call or answering a card; routines that run on their own don't count. What the members of a family agent you
  created write to it also counts for your place. On day 5, we warn you with a notification, in the app and by email if
  you have an account: just write to an agent, or tap "Keep my place". If nobody writes, the shared conversation of
  your family agents and your read-only shares stop when the place is freed. Your account stays open.
- **After the freeing.** Your server is reset and given to someone else: nothing stays on it. We only keep the
  encrypted copy of your place's backups (21 days of history at most: conversations, agent memory, files, Notebook,
  settings), for 30 days at Scaleway, in Paris, then it is erased for good. The key to this copy is then only on your
  phone and in a sealed vault that only your recovery code opens: Kerfy Studio cannot read the copy.
  During those 30 days, "Resume my place" puts it on a new place, if one is free. If you no longer have your phone nor
  your recovery code, it cannot be opened and you start from scratch. Export your data before your place is freed:
  after that, export is no longer possible.
- **End of the beta.** The beta ends on 30 November 2026. Your place then stays free until 31 December 2026. It never
  becomes paid unless you choose a subscription. Without a subscription by that date, your place is freed as described
  above: only its encrypted copy is kept for 30 days, and subscribing during those 30 days brings your agents back. We
  tell you by email and in the app at least 7 days before.
- **No warranty.** It is a test version: features may be missing, change, misbehave or stop, and data may be lost. Keep
  a copy of what matters to you. The beta is provided as is, without warranty, to the extent the law allows.
- **It can change.** We may change or stop the beta, or your participation.
- **How it is handled.** Your data is handled as described in the [privacy policy](privacy.en.md). The [terms of use](terms.en.md)
  and the [acceptable use policy](acceptable-use.en.md) also apply.
- **Installing it.** On iPhone, the beta goes through TestFlight, Apple's testing app; on Android, through Google Play's
  testing programme. Their own terms apply too. The app's publisher and your contact remain Kerfy Studio.
- **Your feedback.** It is welcome, in the app or at **support@bipagents.com**. We may use it to improve BipAgents,
  without owing you anything for it.
